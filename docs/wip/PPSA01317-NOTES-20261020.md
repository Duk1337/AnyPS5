## FPS work: what we know and the next steps

### Root cause found this session
GT7's job threads (Job#0 97% "busy") were spinning on frame-fence words written by RELEASE_MEM on
compute queues 0x20/0x2b. Those submissions were held behind every earlier queue-0 submission by
`Driver::orderReleased` (200–290 ms late). Live flag **`async_unordered`** removes the hold: controls
respond, the car drives at real speed, the race progresses (user: "much more responsive").
Now queue 0 (graphics) is busy ~100% and is the only limit, so queue-0 fixes convert into FPS.
